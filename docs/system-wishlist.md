Recommended Enhancement

Since you're designing a complete HR ecosystem, I recommend implementing the Payroll Management System as part of a larger Human Capital Management (HCM) platform composed of independent modules:

Core HR (Companies, Employees, Departments, Positions)
Leave Management
Attendance & Time Tracking
Payroll Management
Recruitment & Applicant Tracking
Performance Management
Training & Learning Management
Asset Management
Employee Self-Service Portal
Manager Self-Service Portal
HR Administration Portal
Reporting & Analytics
Workflow & Approval Engine
Document Management
Notification Service
API & Integration Layer

This modular architecture allows each subsystem to evolve independently while sharing common services such as authentication, employee records, workflows, notifications, and auditing, making the platform scalable and easier to maintain over time.
